Terms of Service Evaluator

AI-powered analysis of terms of service documents for value alignment and transparency.

Project Overview

The Terms of Service Evaluator is an AI-powered tool designed to analyze terms of service documents and identify potential value misalignments and ethical concerns. This tool helps users understand what they're agreeing to and highlights areas where corporate values might conflict with user interests.

This tool demonstrates how AI can be used to make complex legal documents more accessible and transparent to everyday users, embodying the core principles of the Systems of Value framework.

Key Features

Document Analysis

Automatically analyzes terms of service documents to identify key clauses, potential risks, and value misalignments.

Value Alignment Assessment

Evaluates how well corporate terms align with stated user values and identifies potential conflicts.

Plain Language Translation

Translates complex legal language into understandable terms for everyday users.

Risk Identification

Highlights potential risks and areas of concern that users should be aware of before agreeing to terms.

Transparency & Values

Core Values Embedded in This Tool
  • Transparency: Making complex legal documents accessible
  • User Agency: Empowering users to make informed decisions
  • Fairness: Identifying potential power imbalances
  • Consent: Ensuring users understand what they're agreeing to
  • Dignity: Respecting user autonomy and choice
  • Accountability: Holding corporations accountable for their terms

Technical Implementation

How It Works

The Terms of Service Evaluator uses advanced natural language processing and AI analysis to:

  1. Parse Documents: Extract and structure key clauses from terms of service documents
  2. Value Analysis: Apply predefined value frameworks to assess alignment with user interests
  3. Risk Assessment: Identify potential risks and areas of concern
  4. Plain Language Output: Generate user-friendly summaries and explanations
