Agency

Core Principle
Agency is the capacity to act intentionally and meaningfully within a system. To support agency, systems must enable voluntary participation, informed choice, and reversible decisions.
Agency is not simply freedom from constraint—it is the presence of coherent, supported options that align with a person’s identity, intentions, and moral compass. Aligned systems must empower users to act with understanding and autonomy, not just navigate constraints.

What This Value Requires of Systems
1. Voluntary Participation
o Users should not be coerced or defaulted into participation.
o Terms and engagement should reflect true choice, not friction-based manipulation.
2. Informed Choice
o Options must be clear, contextualized, and comprehensible.
o Systems should reveal trade-offs and consequences of key decisions.
3. Reversibility of Decisions
o People must be able to change their minds. Account deletion, unsubscribing, or withdrawing data should be supported without penalty.
4. Control Over Scope of Engagement
o Users should be able to choose what parts of the system they participate in. This includes data sharing, visibility, and algorithmic targeting.
5. Design Against Manipulation
o Avoid dark patterns, infinite scrolls, and default options that undermine reflective choice.
o Surface control options at decision points.

Examples of Alignment
?? Privacy-Centric Productivity App
* Offers granular control over features.
* Makes consent revocable and encourages user education about their settings.
?? Online Platform with Public Algorithms
* Discloses how recommendations are made.
* Allows users to opt in/out of personalization.

Examples of Misalignment
?? Subscription Platform with Hidden Cancellation
* Auto-renews with no reminders.
* Cancellation is intentionally difficult.
?? Data-Driven App That Requires All-or-Nothing Consent
* Users can only participate by surrendering full data access.
* No meaningful toggles or settings.
?? System That Changes Policies Without Notice
* Users are bound by updates they aren’t informed about.
* Removes agency through opaque shifts.
